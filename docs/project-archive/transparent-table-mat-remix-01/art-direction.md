# Art Direction - Transparent Evidence Line

## Subject
A transparent table mat is difficult to read on camera, so every beat must make the protective surface visible through a real interaction: pouring, wiping, spreading, or rubbing.

## Palette
- `#171412` charcoal: restrained text shadow and contrast.
- `#FFFDF8` warm white: legible Chinese headlines without looking clinical.
- `#E2332E` evidence red: used only to mark a visible contact/result point.
- `#F2C66D` warm gold: small factual kicker tied to the wood table.

## Type personality
Contemporary Chinese Song serif (`Songti SC` with local serif fallbacks) gives the household story a warmer advertising tone while keeping test labels firm and legible. Selected keywords use a restrained warm-gold accent and a short red hand-drawn underline; there is no brush lettering, copied reference typography, or compressed tracking.

## Motion character
The first two seconds use action-matched hard cuts and a short high-stiffness impact spring. The proof beats settle into slower digital pushes. All animation is frame-driven and clamped.

## Layout & rhythm
Copy stays in the upper platform-safe area while hands, liquid, fork, and table edges remain unobstructed. Hook frames are tight; spreading is wide; proof frames return to close interaction.

## Signature device
A thin red evidence line grows toward the visible contact, cleaning path, or returning edge in three proof beats only (`n06`, `n10`, and `n12`). It is not a repeated card or general decoration.

## Scene distinctness
- Hook: liquid/fork/wipe action fragments; job is conflict.
- Spread: full table and person; job is solution reveal.
- Scratch: fork contact close-up; job is friction proof.
- Wipe: sauce and cloth trajectory; job is result proof.

No two consecutive beats share the same primary subject or framing scale.

## Anti-references
- No reference frames, audio, music, subtitles, logo, creator ID, search box, or end card.
- No white brush typography or exact reference shot order.
- No claim of automatic spreading, absolute scratch immunity, oil testing, or certified formaldehyde testing.
- No stock Remotion scene components or generic purple/blue gradients.
